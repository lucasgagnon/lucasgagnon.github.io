Research Interests

Quick links to: my papers on arXiv and a research statement.

My work investigates how counting problems structure objects in algebraic geometry and representation theory. I enojy working in and around combinatorial representation theory, Schubert calculus, and quasisymmetric function theory. Expositions of these areas can be respectively found here, here, and here

I also enjoy mentoring student projects in algebra and combinatorics. If you are an undergraduate looking for a project, get in touch!
