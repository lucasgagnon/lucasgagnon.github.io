Welcome

Mathematical Biography: I am an RTPC (non-tenure track) Assistant Professor at the University of Southern California. My faculty mentor is Sami Assaf. Previously, I was a postdoctoral visitor at York University with Nantel Bergeron. I completed my Ph.D. in 2022 at the University of Colorado Boulder under the supervision of Nat Thiem. Earlier still, I earned my Bachelor of Arts from Macalester College.

Research: I work in algebraic combinatorics and combinatorial representation theory. My research is supported by an AMS-Simons travel grant. To learn about current and past projects, head to my research page.

Teaching: This fall I am teaching Applied Combinatorics (Math 432) at USC. To read more about my teaching experience and philosophy, see my teaching page.
Students can find course materials on Brightspace, or by sending me an email.
